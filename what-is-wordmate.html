What is Wordmate?

Wordmate is a vocabulary app for 44 languages. It has 84 units in 14 practical topics, such as the airport, the hotel and asking directions, at three levels, with spoken pronunciation. English words also come with example sentences. On iPhone and iPad, text-to-speech lets you hear words in the language you are learning. You can keep going from the interactive Home Screen widget or on Apple Watch.

Who is Wordmate for?

People who learn in spare minutes: on the commute, in the coffee line, before a trip, or between classes and meetings. You pick a topic, complete a unit and return exactly where you left off. It also suits people studying more than one language, because the interface language and the learning language are chosen separately and progress is kept independently for each language.

How does Wordmate charge?

Wordmate is a paid App Store download. One purchase includes every language and feature: no subscription and no in-app purchases. For the current price, check the App Store page, because prices differ by country. An Android version is listed on Google Play.

How is Wordmate different from other ways to learn vocabulary?

Based on what the listing describes: from the interactive Home Screen widget you can view a word, move to the next one or save it without opening the app; on Apple Watch you can learn, hear the system voice, save favorites and check progress; words are grouped into practical topics with example sentences for English; and favorites, progress tracking and a learning streak support review. No account is required, there are no third-party ads and no tracking or analytics, and your primary learning data stays on your device.
