Wordmate · FAQ

Can I learn vocabulary in several languages at once, with one purchase and no subscription?

Yes. Wordmate covers vocabulary in 44 languages, and progress is kept independent for each language you study, so you can work on more than one language without mixing them up. One purchase includes every language and feature: no subscription, no in-app purchases.

  1. Pick any of the 44 languages and work through 84 units in 14 practical topics, such as airport, hotel and asking directions, at three levels.
  2. Choose the interface language and the learning language separately.
  3. Each language you study keeps its own progress, so you return exactly where you left off in each one.
  4. On iPhone and iPad, text-to-speech lets you hear pronunciation in the language you are learning. English words also come with example sentences.
  5. Save useful words to favorites for focused review, and build consistency with progress tracking and a learning streak.

How do I keep up with two or more languages on a busy day?

Use spare minutes on your commute, in the coffee line, or between classes and meetings. On iPhone, from the interactive Home Screen widget you can view a word, move to the next one or save it. On Apple Watch you can learn, hear the system voice, save favorites and check your progress.

Do I need an account?

No account is required. No third-party ads and no tracking or analytics. Your primary learning data stays on your device.
