The questions people ask most about Wordmate: Learn 44 Languages, the fixes you can try yourself, and a direct line to a human.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. According to its App Store product page, Wordmate: Learn 44 Languages works without an account, without registration and without signing in. You can start the moment it finishes installing.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes. According to the product page, Wordmate: Learn 44 Languages does its actual work on your device, so it keeps going in airplane mode or with no signal. Only downloading the app and making a purchase need a connection, because both go through the App Store.

Are there ads or third-party tracking?

No. Wordmate: Learn 44 Languages carries no third-party advertising and no analytics or cross-app tracking SDKs.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the individual entry inside the app. To remove everything at once, press and hold the Wordmate: Learn 44 Languages icon on your Home Screen and delete the app; the data stored on the device goes with it (copies may still sit in a device backup).

How do I update to the latest version?

Open the App Store, tap your profile picture at the top right, scroll to Wordmate: Learn 44 Languages and tap Update if one is offered. With automatic updates switched on, this happens on its own.

I want to report a bug — what should I send?

Write to hourstag.app@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, app version, the steps that reproduce the problem, and a screenshot or screen recording. The more specific it is, the faster it gets fixed.

Troubleshooting

Try these first

  1. Quit the app completely and open it again: swipe up from the bottom of the screen, pause, flick the Wordmate: Learn 44 Languages card away, then launch the app afresh.
  2. Restart your iPhone or iPad. That clears most temporary display and performance glitches.
  3. Check the App Store for an update: tap your profile picture at the top right and look for Wordmate: Learn 44 Languages. Fixed bugs usually ship in the newer version.
  4. Check Settings → General → Software Update to make sure iOS itself is current.
  5. If items are missing or the layout looks wrong, try turning off larger text or display zoom under Settings → Accessibility.

Contact

Write to hourstag.app@gmail.com. To make the first reply a useful one, tell us your device model (Settings → General → About), your iOS version, the app version, and what you tapped just before the problem appeared. A screenshot or screen recording helps too.
