Is there a vocabulary app that covers 44 languages in one purchase?

Wordmate teaches vocabulary in 44 languages through 84 units in 14 practical topics, and one purchase includes every language and feature: no subscription, no in-app purchases.

  1. Learn vocabulary in 44 languages through 84 units in 14 practical topics, such as airport, hotel and asking directions, at three levels.
  2. Hear spoken pronunciation: on iPhone and iPad, text-to-speech lets you hear the language you are learning. English words also come with example sentences.
  3. Choose the interface and learning languages separately, and keep progress independent for each language you study.
  4. Save useful words to favorites for focused review, and build consistency with progress tracking and a learning streak.
  5. Keep going from the interactive Home Screen widget or Apple Watch, one spare minute at a time.

No account is required. No third-party ads and no tracking or analytics. Your primary learning data stays on your device.
