What Wordmate: Learn 44 Languages does

Make every spare moment useful with Wordmate, a complete vocabulary app for 44 languages. Structured lessons, natural example sentences, an interactive Home Screen widget and Apple Watch keep practice close at hand—from your phone to your wrist.

ONE APP STORE PURCHASE. EVERYTHING INCLUDED.

Wordmate is a paid download. Purchase it once on the App Store and all 44 languages, every learning level and every feature are included from the start—including the interactive widget and Apple Watch experience. There are no subscriptions, in-app purchases or paid upgrades.

Features at a glance

  • Work through 14 practical topics and 84 focused units. Natural example sentences show how vocabulary works in everyday situations. On iPhone and iPad, text-to-speech (TTS) lets you hear pronunciation in the language you’re learning.
  • Choose the interface and learning languages separately
  • Keep progress independent for each language you study
  • Save useful words to favorites for focused review
  • Build consistency with progress tracking and a learning streak
  • View a word, move to the next one or save it from the interactive Home Screen widget
  • Learn on Apple Watch, hear the system voice, save favorites and check your progress
  • Use Wordmate on your commute, in the coffee line, before a trip or between classes and meetings. Pick a topic, complete a unit and return exactly where you left off.
  • No account is required. There are no third-party ads and no tracking or analytics. Your primary learning data stays on your device.

Privacy at a glance

The short version: no account needed, no personal data collected, no third-party advertising, no third-party analytics.
