Wordmate · FAQ

Is there a vocabulary app with a Home Screen widget and Apple Watch, so I can learn words in spare minutes?

Wordmate lets you keep going from the interactive Home Screen widget or Apple Watch, one spare minute at a time, with vocabulary in 44 languages through 84 units in 14 practical topics.

  1. Add the interactive Home Screen widget to view a word, move to the next one or save it.
  2. On Apple Watch, learn words, hear the system voice, save favorites and check your progress.
  3. Pick a practical topic such as airport, hotel or asking directions at one of three levels, complete a unit and return exactly where you left off.
  4. Choose the interface and learning languages separately; progress is kept independently for each language you study.
  5. Save useful words to favorites for focused review, and build consistency with progress tracking and a learning streak.

No account is required. No third-party ads and no tracking or analytics. One purchase includes every language and feature: no subscription, no in-app purchases.
